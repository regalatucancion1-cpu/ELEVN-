The castle and its setting

Castell de Tamarit is a castle of Romanesque origin dating from the thirteenth century, standing at Tamarit about ten minutes from Tarragona. It faces the sea, set in a wooded landscape, with its own parking.

The venue describes spaces both inside and outside the castle that can be adapted to the shape of the day, from a banquet in the main hall through to dinner in the courtyard, with cocktail areas in the gardens by the sea. The main room is open plan. It is a genuinely rare setting for a wedding in Catalonia: a medieval building on the coast, a short drive from a city, with the Mediterranean in view.

How wedding hours work in Catalonia

Every venue sets its own hours within the rules that apply to it, so the venue is the only reliable source for your date. As background, three separate things sit behind whatever time you are given.

  • Catalan noise rules define the night as beginning at 23:00, with levels measured at the façade of the nearest home rather than at the venue.
  • The model municipal ordinance used across Catalonia commonly caps outdoor amplified music at 23:00, and it covers private grounds as well as public spaces.
  • A licensed banqueting venue typically closes around 02:30, with an extra half hour on Friday and Saturday nights and the eve of public holidays and a further seasonal extension through the summer.

The practical shape that follows across the region is that outdoor spaces carry the ceremony, the aperitif and often dinner, and an indoor room carries the party. It is why so many Catalan venues are built exactly that way.

This is general background on Catalan rules, not a statement about any particular venue. Confirm the hours that apply to your own date directly with the venue, in writing.

Music and sound at the castle

The venue offers a full service that includes the gastronomy, the decoration, the lighting and the music, so everything can be arranged through them if that is what you want.

Couples who would like to bring a particular DJ are best raising it at the point of booking, so it is agreed and written into the arrangement from the start rather than added later. The same conversation should settle who is providing the sound system on the night, which is all a visiting DJ needs to know in order to plan properly. Agreed early, it is a straightforward thing to arrange.

An outdoor ceremony by the sea

A ceremony in the gardens with the Mediterranean behind it is one of the reasons couples choose this venue, and it is worth giving its own sound rather than borrowing whatever is set up for later in the day.

Outdoors there are no walls to help carry a voice, and on any coastal site there is usually a breeze through the afternoon. What we bring for a ceremony is coverage placed close to the guests rather than a single source at the front, and microphones with proper wind protection for the celebrant, the readings and the couple, so the people at the back hear the ceremony as clearly as the people at the front.

Getting your guests there and home

Tarragona is about ten minutes away and has plenty of accommodation, Reus airport is close, and Barcelona and its airport are roughly an hour and a quarter by road. There is parking at the castle.

For a wedding running late, one piece of advice that applies anywhere: book more than one coach back. Two departures thirty or forty minutes apart let the guests who want an early night take the first and the guests who came to dance stay to the end, instead of everybody leaving at the same moment.

Frequently asked questions

Where is it and how do guests get there?
At Tamarit, about ten minutes from Tarragona, facing the sea, with parking on site. Reus airport is close and Barcelona is roughly an hour and a quarter.

What spaces are there?
An open-plan main room inside the castle plus outdoor areas including a courtyard and gardens by the sea. Confirm the detail with the venue for your date.

Can we bring our own DJ?
The venue offers a full service including music. If you would like a particular DJ, raise it when you book so it can be agreed from the start, along with who provides the sound system.

What time do weddings finish in Catalonia?
It is set per venue and per municipality. As background, the legal night starts at 23:00, outdoor music is commonly capped at 23:00, and licensed venues typically close around 02:30 with Friday, Saturday and summer extensions.
